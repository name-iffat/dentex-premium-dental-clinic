# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
People exploring dental care in Malaysia, comparing treatments and finding a way to contact the clinic on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose
Present Dentex's care, team and services clearly and make consultation enquiries easy to find.

## Brand Personality
Bold, modern and expressive, as requested by the user. Keep the experience reassuring and approachable.

## Anti-references
Disconnected blur effects, decorative clutter, and unfinished template copy.

## Design Principles
Give the first screen a memorable identity. Use smile-inspired curves as a recurring visual motif. Preserve existing photography and typography. Keep decoration secondary to information and booking actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Decorative graphics are hidden from assistive technology. Respect reduced motion, keep keyboard focus visible, and prevent decoration from covering content on small screens.
